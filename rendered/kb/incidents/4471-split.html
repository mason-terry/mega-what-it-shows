  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out with day and month swapped, through the app, by email, and through booking-v2’s form. This is the bug #4471 describes.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. It only comes in through the form, only from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs a hint that matches the tenant’s locale.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not a swap. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” That reads as a time-zone problem: a date turned into an instant and back in another zone. The desk had already noted these reports (desk-notes.json) and left them filed under parser.
